The Good, the Bad, and the IMPL-EX

2026  |  Numerical methods  |  IMPL-EX integration scheme

A practical guide to the IMPL-EX (IMPLicit-EXplicit) integration scheme for finite element analysis. Covers the math behind the method, a toy model demonstration, contact treatment, and a real application to seismic pounding in masonry structures. For earthquake engineers dealing with convergence failures from contact and material softening.
